Project Overview

Goal: Build machine-learning potential energy surfaces (ML-PES) for Criegee intermediates (carbonyl oxides), run ML-MD trajectories, and compute IR spectra — with multi-state CASSCF electronic corrections for atmospheric photochemistry applications.

CH₂OO

5-atom carbonyl oxide. Benchmark molecule for the Coulomb+KRR pipeline. B3LYP/6-31G* reference. Best model: 344 frames, 0.64 kcal/mol validation error.

MVKO

Methyl vinyl ketone oxide, (CH₂=CH)(CH₃)COO, C₄H₆O₂, 12 atoms, 30 vibrational modes. Criegee intermediate from MVK ozonolysis. Atmospherically relevant.

ML-PES Backend

Coulomb matrix descriptor + Kernel Ridge Regression (KRR). StandardScaler on features and targets is critical. Force training disabled; forces via finite differences. Succeeded by MACE for IR work.

Electronic Corrections

CASSCF(2,2)/(4,4) + NEVPT2 delta-ML in normal-mode coordinate space. Four MVKO conformers (syn/anti × trans/cis) — all grids complete.

Gamma Sweep Finding

Softer γ reduces unphysical Hessian modes (5→3 of 9) but harms IR spectrum quality (less accurate forces). Hessian stiffness is intrinsic to Coulomb+RBF second derivatives — not fixable by hyperparameter tuning. This ultimately motivated the move to MACE.

MVKO — New Molecule

Methyl vinyl ketone oxide: (CH₂=CH)(CH₃)COO, C₄H₆O₂, 12 atoms, 30 vibrational modes. Criegee intermediate from ozonolysis of methyl vinyl ketone (MVK). Strong permanent COO dipole (~4.5 D) dominates ACF IR — see IR Spectra.

Atom Ordering

Fixed ordering required (Coulomb matrix not permutation-invariant): C1(Criegee), O1(proximal), O2(distal), C2(vinyl=CH-), C3(=CH2), C4(methyl), H1(on C2), H2(on C3), H3(on C3), H4(CH3), H5(CH3), H6(CH3)

n_desc = 78 (vs 15 for CH₂OO). Use γ ~5× smaller than CH₂OO.

Adaptive Sampling

Critical lesson: Adding high-energy PSI4 frames to the KRR ML-PES shifts the kernel allocation away from equilibrium, displacing the ML-PES minimum from the PSI4 equilibrium → imaginary NM modes → saddle-point minimizer → broken IR spectrum.

What Happened

The 1300-frame adaptive model (outputs/adaptive_production_20260324b/mlpes_adaptive_final.pkl) trained with 1000–2000 K PSI4 MD frames developed 3 imaginary NM modes at the PSI4 equilibrium (−1285, −880, −718 cm⁻¹). The pre-minimizer overshoots to a saddle point; ZPE initialization amplifies imaginary-mode velocities to ~1619 K.

Rules Going Forward

  • For ZPE-floor IR spectra: always use the near-equilibrium model (mlpes_initial.pkl, 904 frames) with --preminimize + ZPE-filter [50, 4000] cm⁻¹
  • For future adaptive runs: include the PSI4-optimized geometry (forces ≈ 0) as an anchor row when adding high-energy frames
  • Never use ML-MD (only PSI4 MD) to generate adaptive sampling candidates
  • Adaptive model is suitable for high-energy force/energy prediction, not for IR

ZPE Initialization

ZPE init is ESSENTIAL for MVKO IR spectra. ZPE energy per mode at 893 cm⁻¹ equals ~1280 K of classical thermal energy. Without explicit ZPE initialization (--no-zpe-init), only torsional modes (<400 cm⁻¹) are populated at 300 K.

Analytic KRR Forces & Hessian (March 2026)

Implemented in modules/bakken.py (MLPESDriver):

  • analytic_forces(coords): F = 2γ σ_y J_scaledᵀ g — 903× faster than FD, matches to 3.3×10⁻⁵ Ha/Å
  • analytic_hessian(coords): exact H matching FD to <5 cm⁻¹
  • Finding: analytic and FD Hessians give identical unphysical frequencies — the problem is intrinsic to Coulomb+RBF curvature

CASSCF Delta-ML Corrections

Approach Progression

1. Coulomb-Space CASSCF Delta-ML (March 2026) — Failed

Near-eq: δ = +0.62 ± 1.05 kcal/mol — negligible. Coulomb-descriptor KRR failed because all MVKO 300 K geometries have kernel K≈0.999 (heavy-atom charges dominate; high-energy outliers bleed into equilibrium). 1D spline also failed (frame-29 anomaly δ = −19 kcal/mol at ΔE=27).

2. Normal-Mode Coordinate Delta-ML (April 2026)

Fix (casscf_nm_delta.py): KRR in normal-mode coordinate space q = U_vibᵀ ⋅ M½ ⋅ (R − R_ref). q = 0 at equilibrium; ||q||² grows monotonically with distortion. High-energy outliers have large ||q|| → no bleeding.

Class: NMKRRDeltaModel. Integrated into ir_md_spectrum.py via NMDeltaDriver and --nm-delta-model flag.

3. NEVPT2 Correction (April 2026) — Marginal

PySCF CASSCF(4,4)/6-31G* + SC-NEVPT2 (~80 s/frame). 14/50 training frames corrupted by CASSCF state-switching (removed). Clean model LOO-CV: 1.60 kcal/mol (36 frames, γ=0.01, α=1e-5).

Physical relevance: ozonolysis-energized MVKO (~50 kcal/mol internal energy, T_eff ≈ 840 K) accesses geometries where NEVPT2 corrects B3LYP by −9 to −21 kcal/mol. Superseded by the conformer CASSCF grid approach.

MACE Backend

Motivation

Root cause of all IR failures: Coulomb+KRR AND sGDML both use 1/r-based descriptors whose second derivatives are intrinsically stiff under RBF/Matérn kernels → C-H modes at 10,000–38,000 cm⁻¹ (physical: ~3,000 cm⁻¹). This cannot be fixed by hyperparameter tuning.

sGDML (April 2026) — Failed

  • Same 1/r descriptor stiffness as Coulomb+KRR (38,342 cm⁻¹ max C-H mode)
  • Multi-T+NM training data violates sGDML path-integral coherence assumption (89× force-integral mismatch)
  • Kernel memory: 23.5 GB at n_train=900, must cap at 300
  • With use_E_cstr + dE<5 filter: near-eq RMSE 0.69 kcal/mol but 192 kcal/mol on full set, 8 imaginary modes, MD dissociates at step 40

MACE (Multi-Atomic Cluster Expansion)

SO(3)-equivariant message-passing neural network with local atomic energy decomposition + force-weighted training → physical Hessian by design.

ParameterValue
Architecturehidden_irreps='64x0e + 64x1o', r_max=5.0 Å, 2 layers

IR Spectra

ACF-Based IR: Failure for MVKO

ACF-based IR from MD FAILS for MVKO. All attempts — Coulomb+KRR, CH-retrained, MACE, B3LYP and wB97X dipole surfaces, NMDipoleSurface (R²=0.9997), varying ZPE leakage, 5×30,000-step trajectories — produce spectra dominated by ~150 cm⁻¹ torsional features with zero fingerprint signal.

Root cause: MVKO has a ~4.5 D permanent dipole from the COO zwitterion. Torsional motion reorients the permanent dipole → dominates the ACF. The ratio IACF(ωtors) / I(ωstretch) ≈ 70,000:1. This is a physical property of the molecule, not a bug. Not fixable by thermostat tuning, ZPE settings, or a better dipole surface.

Correct Approach: Harmonic Dipole Derivatives

Use NMDipoleSurface.ir_intensities(eq_coords) = ||∂μ/∂q_k||² (Debye²/amu·Bohr²). Harmonic IR absorption intensity per mode, equivalent to the QM transition dipole squared. No ACF, no MD required. 
from modules.nm_pes import load_dipole_surface
ds  = load_dipole_surface('outputs/ir_nm_pes_anti_cis_300K/dipole_surface.pkl')
eq  = np.load('outputs/anti_cis_nm_pes_20260513/eq_coords.npy')
freqs = ds.freqs_vib          # (30,) — normal-mode frequencies
ints  = ds.ir_intensities(eq) # (30,) — relative IR intensities

Hot-Molecule IR Emission (July 2026)

Extended from equilibrium IR to hot-molecule emission relevant to ozonolysis-energized MVKO (~50 kcal/mol internal energy, Teff ≈ 840 K).

Method

  • Multi-conformer population weighting: Boltzmann over wB97X-D/6-31G* conformer energies
  • CASSCF-corrected MACE PES for each conformer
  • Harmonic dipole derivatives at each conformer equilibrium geometry (LHA)
  • Spectra summed with Boltzmann weights

Local Harmonic Approximation (LHA)

The LHA computes the IR spectrum as ||∂μ/∂q_k||² evaluated at the ML-PES-minimized geometry for each conformer. This is correct for MVKO because (a) the permanent dipole reorientation problem eliminates ACF, and (b) near-equilibrium geometry gives the dominant absorption intensity. The 4-panel LHA conceptual figure was added to clarify this vs. the ACF approach.

Comparison with Experiment

Theory-experiment comparison against digitized Chung & Lee (2021) experimental MVKO spectrum. Anti-cis (predominant conformer) and syn-trans spectra both computed and combined with Boltzmann weighting. Conformer figure (outputs/mvko_conformers_figure.png) shows wB97X-D/6-31G* and Barber CCSD(T) conformer energies alongside CASSCF S1/T1 gaps.
