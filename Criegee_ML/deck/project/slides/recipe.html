What it took

The necessary ingredients for a spectroscopic surface

1 · Clean single-state data

No root flipping between neighbours; checked by two starts per point

2 · The right method for the well

CCSD(T), with a 9-D larger-basis Δ; tested along cuts against NEVPT2

3 · A fit judged on held-out batches

0.6 meV on unseen geometries, with the harmonics checked at every fit

4 · Every coordinate free

Rock, wag and C–H stretches shift C–O and the scissor by tens of cm⁻¹

5 · Quantum nuclei

VCI for levels; TRPMD where VCI cannot reach, calibrated against it

6 · Rotation

Band shapes are rotational contours; level-dependent A, B, C matter

7 · Tests beyond the data it was fitted to

Isotopologues, microwave constants, CCSD(T) where the beads go, and a second dynamics method on the same surface

The lessons of the deck as a checklist. Each was learned by a failure: 1 from the SA4 data (5-50 meV scatter) and the NEVPT2 two-start disagreements; 2 from the NEVPT2 upturn on the outer O-O wall and the aug-cc-pVDZ softening; 3 from the neural-network ensemble (28 meV between members at the minimum) and from the earlier MVKO kernel surfaces with modes above 5000 cm-1; 4 from the 4-D model's scissor shift of -6 against -42 and the restraint springs mixing with O-O; 5 from classical band centres 6-30 cm-1 above the quantum levels; 6 from single trajectories giving P/R doublets; 7 is what makes the agreement believable rather than tuned: nothing was adjusted to the CD2OO spectrum or the microwave constants.
