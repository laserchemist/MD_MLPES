J. M. Smith · Temple University · 22 · ORCA 6.1.1, RHF-CCSD(T), frozen core; order-5 Cs-symmetric polynomial, 1192 terms

Coordinates: r(O-O), angle COO, r(C-O), both r(C-H), both HCO angles and both out-of-plane dihedrals; the polynomial is even in the out-of-plane coordinates, so the Cs symmetry is exact. Data: a 163-point finite-difference Hessian at the centre (batch 0), then six batches of 500 points by farthest-point sampling of a harmonic normal-mode pool, then two batches of 225 from well9d_extend (next slide). Held-out error is computed by leaving each batch out in turn, so it measures prediction at new geometries, not interpolation. Basis set: the difference aug-cc-pVTZ minus aug-cc-pVDZ at 250 points is fitted by an order-3 polynomial (held-out 4.3 meV over its range, 1.05 meV near the minimum) and added before the fit. Dipole: bond-vector model fitted to finite-field CCSD(T) dipoles (field 0.001 au) at 500 points. No restraint springs anywhere: all nine coordinates are free, unlike the 3-D and 4-D models earlier in the deck. Cost, for scale: the 400 extension points took 11 minutes with 8 ORCA jobs on a MacBook Pro, and 50 aug-cc-pVTZ points took 21 minutes.
