Refinement 3: DVR in practice

H = −(ħ²/2) Σ ∂ᵢ Gij(q) ∂ⱼ + V(q)

  • G: inverse kinetic metric, translations and rotations removed (J = 0)
  • Sinc DVR: V diagonal on the grid; kinetic term Σ Dᵢᵀ G Dⱼ
  • 28³ grid (3-D) or 20⁴ (4-D); lowest 16 states in 5–30 s
  • Assigned by overlap with harmonic states; intensity ν |⟨0|μ|v⟩|²
