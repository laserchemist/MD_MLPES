Earlier summary: four findings from the 3-D and 4-D work

1 · The surface set the band positions

The NN ensemble could not fix the curvature at the minimum, and the SA4 reference data scattered 5–50 meV between points.

2 · A clean grid fixes the data

State-specific CASSCF+NEVPT2 grid: fit residual 0.3 meV. Quantum O–O fundamental 937 cm⁻¹ (3-D), 944 (4-D); experiment 908.

3 · The band shape is rotational

Each trajectory gives a P/R doublet at ν₀ ± ω_rot; 160 trajectories give a smooth envelope like experiment’s.

4 · CCSD(T) gets the anharmonicity

4-D CCSD(T): O–O shift −28 (VPT2 −24). With aug-cc-pVTZ harmonics, O–O 907 and C–O 1281 vs 908 and 1286.
