Toward one surface · what the switch costs

A ramp, not a barrier, and most of it need not be switched

J. M. Smith · Temple University · 37 · 29_aug method check: CCSD(T) and NEVPT2, both aug-cc-pVDZ

The Delta figure plots Delta in meV over a short O-O range, which makes the switch look like a cliff. On the surface's own scale (panel A) it removes about 0.13-0.15 eV (3 kcal/mol) over 0.2 A, while the bond climbs about 2 eV. What trajectories feel is force (panel B): up to about 1.2 eV/A added where the bond force is about 3.3 eV/A, roughly a third, which is enough to change how fast a hot O-O bond stretches. The fix (panel C): most of Delta here is not CCSD(T) versus NEVPT2 at all. The method cut has state-specific NEVPT2 and CCSD(T) in the same aug-cc-pVDZ basis, and their difference is under 5 meV in the well (it grows to about 30 meV at 1.52 A, partly the state-specific upturn). The rest, about 100 meV, is basis and orbital (cc-pVDZ SA4 to aug-cc-pVDZ), and both sides of that are multireference, so it is valid at any O-O and needs no switch. Plan: learn Delta_MR from a NEVPT2 aug-cc-pVDZ cut out to dissociation (about 20 points, still to be measured; the dotted line is a placeholder), apply it everywhere, and switch only Delta_CC. A wider switch alone does not help much here because Delta is still changing at the edge of the data.
