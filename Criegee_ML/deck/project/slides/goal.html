Unimolecular decay to OH depends on how much energy the Criegee carries (Lester & Klippenstein, Acc. Chem. Res. 51, 978, 2018). As molecules grow, experiments thin out: predictions need a surface we trust at high energy, checked first where the data are rich.

J. M. Smith · Temple University · 4

The pipeline is meant to carry over: build the surface from a cheap multireference method everywhere plus a coupled-cluster correction where it is valid, check it against spectroscopy, then run dynamics. CH2OO is where every step can be checked. Chem. Res. 51, 978 (2018), prompt and thermal decay of Criegee intermediates to OH.
