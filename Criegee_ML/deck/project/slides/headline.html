Three independent checks on the same surface: exact quantum vibrations (VCI) with rotational contours, ring-polymer dynamics converged in bead number, and the isotopologues CD₂OO, ¹³CH₂OO, CH₂¹⁸O¹⁸O and CHDOO. The one-slide answer. Surface: CCSD(T)/aug-cc-pVDZ at 3,613 geometries in all nine internal coordinates, plus a 9-D aug-cc-pVTZ minus aug-cc-pVDZ correction from 250 points, fitted as an order-5 Cs-symmetric polynomial (held-out error 0.59 meV within 0.8 eV of the minimum). Fundamentals (VCI, cm-1) against Huang et al. 2015 (0.25 cm-1 resolution): nu3 1432.3 / 1434.1, nu4 1275.1 / 1285.9, nu5 1202.5 / 1213.3, nu6 910.0 / 909.26, nu8 853.4 / 847.44; mean absolute error 6.0 cm-1. Published benchmark: Li, Carter, Bowman, Dawes, Xie and Guo, J. Phys. Chem. Lett. 5, 2364 (2014), CCSD(T)-F12a/aug-cc-pVTZ, more than 50,000 points, VCI-5 in MULTIMODE: mean absolute error 6.5 cm-1 on the same five bands (values from Chang et al., J. Chem. Phys. 146, 244302 (2017), Table II). The 0.04% is the largest error in the ground-state constants when the vibrational corrections from our surface are added to the semi-experimental equilibrium structure of McCarthy et al.; with the surface's own minimum the constants are about 1% low (bonds slightly long at aug-cc-pVDZ), so the structure, not the vibration-rotation physics, carries the remaining error. Band contour: correlation 0.91 between the computed and measured O-O band, 860-960 cm-1.
