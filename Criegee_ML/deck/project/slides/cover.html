Research update · November 2026

Status deck: why hot Criegee intermediates matter, the refinements that led to a spectroscopically accurate CH2OO surface (9-D CCSD(T), checked by VCI, ring-polymer dynamics, isotopologues and microwave constants), how this compares with the published VCI, and what carries over to MVK-oxide. Structures from the ACS Fall 2025 poster (Simiyu and Smith): the primary ozonide from C2H4 + O3 falls apart to CH2OO + H2CO. The mechanism, ozone adding across the C=C bond to a primary ozonide that cleaves to a carbonyl and a carbonyl oxide, is Criegee's: R. Criegee, "Mechanism of Ozonolysis," Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. Engl. 14, 745-752 (1975); the carbonyl oxide carries his name. Every number comes from the runs listed on the last slide.
