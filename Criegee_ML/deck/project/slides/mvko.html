Left column from the MD_MLPES talk (https://laserchemist.github.io/MD_MLPES/talk/): Coulomb-matrix KRR, KRR in normal-mode coordinates and sGDML all put 5-12 of MVKO's 30 modes above 5000 cm-1; the reference data were B3LYP and wB97X-D/6-31G*; the dipole autocorrelation spectrum of MVKO was dominated by features near 150 cm-1 (permanent-dipole rotation and torsion) with no fingerprint signal, so the fingerprint came from harmonic dipole derivatives; an adaptive loop that added 1000-2000 K frames improved training error but spoiled the surface near equilibrium. Experiment to aim at: Chung and Lee 2021, step-scan FTIR, seven bands B1-B7 between 930 and 1460 cm-1 (syn-trans and anti-cis); Barber, Pandit, Green, Trongsiriwat, Walsh, Klippenstein and Lester 2018, IR action spectrum in the C-H overtone region with VPT2 (B2PLYP-D3/cc-pVTZ) assignments. What does not carry over unchanged: a full 30-mode VCI is far larger than 9-mode; the plan is GVPT2 for all modes, reduced-dimension VCI on the fingerprint modes, and TRPMD for the full molecule, calibrated where VCI is possible, as done here for CH2OO. A CCSD(T) point for C4H6O2 costs far more than one for CH2OO (CCSD(T) scales as the seventh power of system size), so the CCSD(T) is applied as a Delta on a cheaper surface rather than as the whole surface.
