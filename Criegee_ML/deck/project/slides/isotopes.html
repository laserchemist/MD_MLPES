Vibrational corrections right to 0.04% for all 27 constants

The 1% is the structure: r(O–O) 0.009 Å long at aug-cc-pVDZ, the same cause as ν₄ and ν₅ sitting 11 cm⁻¹ low

J. M. Smith · Temple University · 28 · Nakajima & Endo, JCP 139, 101103 (2013); McCarthy et al., JPCL 4, 4133 (2013)

Ground-state rotational constants of nine isotopologues from Fourier-transform microwave spectroscopy: Nakajima and Endo 2013 and McCarthy, Cheng, Crabtree, Martinez, Nguyen, Womack and Stanton 2013, who also derived a semi-experimental equilibrium structure (r(O-O) 1.3405 A, r(C-O) 1.2689 A, angle COO 117.91 deg). Six of the nine species shown; 13C, 18O and CHD variants not shown behave the same (largest error 0.041% over all 27 constants). Left column: equilibrium constants from our surface's own minimum plus our vibrational correction. Right column: the same vibrational correction added to the semi-experimental structure. The surface minimum has r(O-O) 1.3498 A (+0.009), r(C-O) 1.2748 A (+0.006): bonds slightly long, typical of aug-cc-pVDZ with frozen core. Because the vibrational corrections are right, the remaining error is a structure error that a core-valence and complete-basis correction at the minimum should remove; it is also the likely cause of the 11 cm-1 deficit in nu4 and nu5. Isotopic substitution also changes the vibrational modes: the C-D stretches move from 3191/3032 to 2416/2211 cm-1 (next slide).
