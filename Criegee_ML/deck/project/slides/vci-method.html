Vibrational configuration interaction · stage well9d_vci

VCI in practice, on the 9-D surface

H = ½ Σ Pk² + ½ Σ (Πα−πα) μαβ (Πβ−πβ) − ⅛ Σ μαα + V(Q)

  • Watson Hamiltonian in Eckart normal modes, with Coriolis coupling
  • V as an n-mode expansion to 3-mode terms; dipole to 2-mode
  • Basis: low modes to 7000 cm⁻¹ × C–H stretches to 3 quanta, 36,890 states
  • Larger basis changes fundamentals by ≤ 4 cm⁻¹, zero-point by 0.03

Then rotation (well9d_bands)

A, B, C for every level from ⟨μ⟩ plus Coriolis terms

Asymmetric-rotor line strengths, 300 K

43 bands, 2.3 million lines, 22 s

J. M. Smith · Temple University · 25 · Watson, Mol. Phys. 15, 479 (1968); n-mode: Carter, Culik & Bowman, JCP 107, 10458 (1997)

Code: diabml/vci.py, workflow/vci9.py, rotor.py. The same approach as MULTIMODE (Bowman and Carter), written for this project so it can read our surfaces directly. Potential grids: 174,989 points; where the polynomial is used outside its fitted range it is guarded by the harmonic value, which changed nothing measurable. Sparse matrix solved for the lowest eigenvalues with ARPACK; about 5 minutes on a laptop. Assignments by overlap with the product basis; the fundamentals have 69-96% of a single basis state except the C-H symmetric stretch, which sits in a dense polyad near 3030 cm-1. Intensities from 2-mode dipole matrix elements, in km/mol. Zero-point energy 6626.7 cm-1 (harmonic 6717.2, GVPT2 6614.6). Rotational constants: ground state 2.5618, 0.41158, 0.35388 cm-1 against experiment 2.5934, 0.41579, 0.35763; for the band shapes the ground state is anchored to experiment and every other level takes its computed change from the ground state.
