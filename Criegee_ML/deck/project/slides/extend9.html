J. M. Smith · Temple University · 23 · 400 new CCSD(T)/aVDZ + 50 from rpmd_check; 50 more aVTZ points for the Δ

Why: path-integral beads spread over the zero-point width of each atom, about 0.16 A for H, so a 300 K ring polymer visits C-H bond lengths the 0.8 eV fit never saw. rpmd_check computed CCSD(T) at bead geometries and found errors growing with energy; well9d_extend then chose 400 new points by farthest-point sampling from the saved bead geometries (half) and a harmonic sample weighted toward 2 eV (half), spread away from everything already computed, and added the 50 rpmd_check points. The surface before extension, tested at these points it had never seen: RMS 1.5 meV at 0.4-0.8 eV, 4.9 at 0.8-1.0, 16.8 at 1.0-1.5, 40.9 at 1.5-2.0 eV. After refitting with the window raised to 1.5 eV, the held-out error within 0.8 eV is unchanged (0.61 to 0.59 meV), the harmonic frequencies of the low modes move by 2 cm-1 or less, and the C-H stretches by 7 and 11 cm-1. The new rpmd_check, at P = 128: 0.6% of bead geometries outside the fitted range; error 4.1 meV RMS there within the 1.5 eV window, 25 meV beyond it (a rare tail); weighted by how often the beads go there, at most about 2 meV. Before the extension the share outside was 10.9% (P = 16). The TRPMD band centres did not change, which confirms the tail does not matter for the spectrum.
