Isotopologue spectra need no new electronic structure: only the masses change, so this is a test of the surface away from the CH2OO data it was judged on. Experimental band positions as quoted by Li 2020, Table I, from Huang et al. 2016 (our digitized Q-branch maxima agree to about 1 cm-1: 852.8, 1054.5, 1317.2). Mode labels follow Huang and Li for CD2OO (nu4 = OCD trans bend / O-O stretch at 1054, nu5 = OCD cis bend at 1017), which differ from the CH2OO parents: our 1010.6 band descends from the CH2OO C-O stretch and our 1051.2 band from the rock. Grey bands in the figure were excluded from the correlation (interference in the experiment). In CD2OO the C-O stretch mixes strongly with the CD2 scissor (the nu3 parent overlap is only 0.34), which is why the highest band carries the C-O error. Published VCI for CD2OO: J. Li, "Ro-vibrational spectra of the simplest deuterated Criegee intermediate CD2OO," Chin. J. Chem. Phys. 33, 65-68 (2020), doi 10.1063/1674-0068/cjcp1911195, VSCF/VCI-5 on the 2014 surface: 863.1, 1013.7, 1063.3, 1318.3 cm-1; Li notes that measured and computed intensities disagree strongly for nu3 (intensity borrowing from nu6 + nu7 and 2nu8), as ours do. Li also gives rotational constants of every v = 1 level (Table II) that can be compared with ours.
