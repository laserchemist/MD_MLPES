Motivation · ozonolysis

Ozonolysis makes CH₂OO hot enough to leave the well

C₂H₄ + O₃ forms CH₂OO with up to 48 kcal/mol (about 2.1 eV) above its minimum, the dashed line.

That is above the barriers out of the well, so part of the CH₂OO reacts before collisions can cool it.

Hot molecules sample geometries far from the minimum, where a harmonic or well-only surface does not hold.

Ozonolysis mechanism: Criegee, Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. Engl. 14, 745 (1975). Energies relative to the CH2OO minimum, from the HEAT-345(Q) study of Nguyen, Lee, Matthews, McCarthy and Stanton (J. Phys. Chem. A 2015, 119, 5524-5533), as drawn on our ACS Fall 2025 poster. Ozonolysis releases a broad distribution of energy into CH2OO: some is collisionally stabilized, some decays promptly (to dioxirane over TS 1, and to other products). 48 kcal/mol is about 2.1 eV, comparable to the O-O dissociation region of the NEVPT2 surface later in the deck and above the 0.4-1.8 eV window of the new CCSD(T) shell. That is the regime the surface must handle for ozonolysis-energy IR and branching. Two cautions from Kuwata's 2024 perspective (J. Phys. Chem. A 128, 7331): Nguyen et al. attribute nearly all the OH from ethene ozonolysis to primary-ozonide diradicals rather than to CH2OO, and a significant fraction of small Criegees is formed cold or collisionally stabilized (Kroll et al. 2001). Thermal (stabilised) decomposition of CH2OO and CD2OO, measured directly: Stone, Au, Sime, Medeiros, Blitz, Seakins, Decker and Sheps, Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys. 20, 24940 (2018), cavity-enhanced UV absorption at 450-650 K with MESMER fits: barrier to decomposition (via dioxirane) 81.8 kJ/mol for CH2OO and 80.1 for CD2OO, products 63.7% H2 + CO2, 36.0% H2O + CO and only 0.3% OH + HCO; CD2OO decomposes faster than CH2OO. These are the well-exit channels a surface for hot CH2OO has to reach.
