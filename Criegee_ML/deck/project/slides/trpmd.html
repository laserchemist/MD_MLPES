J. M. Smith · Temple University · 32 · intensity-weighted band centres, cm⁻¹, 300 K; TRPMD: Rossi, Ceriotti & Manolopoulos, JCP 140, 234116 (2014)

Thermostatted ring-polymer molecular dynamics (TRPMD): each atom is a ring of P beads joined by springs; the internal modes of the ring are thermostatted (friction 2 lambda omega_k, lambda 0.5) and the spectrum is the Fourier transform of the Kubo-transformed, bead-averaged dipole autocorrelation. Same surface and dipole as the VCI, so differences are the dynamics alone. Classical reference: 160 trajectories from the ir stage; band centres are intensity-weighted over 870-950, 1250-1340 and 1400-1500 cm-1 with bootstrap error bars (TRPMD errors 0.2-0.6 cm-1). VCI centres are the centres of the VCI contour over the same windows. A friction scan (lambda 0 to 1) changed nothing. TRPMD is known to leave part of the anharmonic shift and to broaden bands through the thermostat (Rossi et al. 2014; Benson and Althorpe 2021 for quasi-centroid MD); here it is quantified on a molecule where the exact answer is available. Cost scales with the number of atoms times P, not exponentially with modes as VCI does: the reason it is the route to MVK-oxide. P = 128 took about 4 hours for 64 trajectories; P = 32 is enough here (planning stage rpmd_plan predicts this from the harmonic frequencies).
