Done since the 4 November version: the 9-D CCSD(T) surface with a 9-D aug-cc-pVTZ correction and CCSD(T) dipole; VCI with Coriolis coupling; rotational band shapes; isotopologue microwave constants and CD2OO; classical and TRPMD spectra on the same surface; CCSD(T) checks at bead geometries and the extension to 1.5 eV. The CH2OO result is complete enough to publish (an optional refinement for the paper: core-valence and CBS corrections at the minimum, for nu4 and nu5); the ingredients list is what carries to MVK-oxide. For MVKO the first decision is the cheap base surface (wB97X-D or B3LYP, aug basis) and how many CCSD(T) Delta points per conformer; the CH2OO numbers (250 points for a 9-D Delta to 4 meV) give a starting estimate. The 13 kJ/mol emission is already computed by VCI for CH2OO (hot bands, T_vib 691 K) and is ready to compare with the emission experiment. For hot molecules, the kinetic benchmark is Stone et al., PCCP 20, 24940 (2018): decomposition rates of CH2OO and CD2OO over 450-650 K and the product branching, a test of the surface at the dioxirane barrier (about 80 kJ/mol, 0.8 eV) and of the isotope effect.
