The deck's spine. Each step was forced by a specific failure of the step before, and each later section of the deck backs one row. Values in the chart (cm-1, quantum fundamentals against 909.3, 1285.9, 1434.1): 3-D state-specific NEVPT2/cc-pVDZ (26_gs): O-O 937, C-O 1365, no scissor in the model (mean of two bands); 4-D NEVPT2 (27_gs4d): 944, 1331, 1513; 4-D CCSD(T)/aug-cc-pVDZ plus aug-cc-pVTZ Delta (31_cc4d, DVR): 923, 1279, 1478; 9-D CCSD(T) plus 9-D aug-cc-pVTZ Delta with VCI (31_cc4d, well9d stages): 910.0, 1275.1, 1432.3. A further step, extending the 9-D surface to 1.5 eV where the ring-polymer beads go, changed none of the fundamentals by more than 2 cm-1 and is shown later with the TRPMD check. Lessons that were not refinements but ruled options out: NEVPT2 with state-specific orbitals turns up on the outer O-O wall (method_cut), and aug-cc-pVDZ alone softens O-O; both are in the appendix.
